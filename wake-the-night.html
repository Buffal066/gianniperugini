01 / THE ATTITUDE

COLD FORM.
WILD SOUL.

Some things are made to blend in.
This one was imagined for the dark.

GLACE NOIRE brings ice, metal, and a flash of fire into a fictional energy-drink identity. A raven at its heart. An unmistakable presence.

A brand concept by Gianni Perugini. Montréal.

Visual description

A frosted black can stands between glowing pumpkins. A pale hand with glossy black nails presses the red raven emblem. Cold white smoke pours around the can. GLACE NOIRE remains visible. The audio is atmospheric; there is no intelligible speech.

04 / BEHIND THE BRAND

AN IDEA.

From a can design to a world of its own.

GLACE NOIRE is an independent portfolio project by Montréal designer and AI creative Gianni Perugini. The original packaging artwork sets the direction; a film created in Google Flow brings that identity into motion.

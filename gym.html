01 / THE CONCEPT

A COLDER WORLD.
A CLEARER
STATE OF MIND.

What if a gym brand could feel like the moment just before a lift?

Glace Noire explores that tension. Cold surfaces, suspended water, and deliberate movement create a world where focus takes centre stage.

This independent concept brings together three image studies and three short films. A visual identity explored through atmosphere, rather than a real-world gym or membership offer.
